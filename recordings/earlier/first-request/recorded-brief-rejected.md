We checked 13 Microsoft 365 accounts as of 21 November 2026 for security gaps and unused licences.

**Security findings**

1. **Administrators without a second sign-in step (MFA): 2 accounts** (user-004, user-005). An administrator account protected only by a password is the easiest way for someone to take over the company's email and files.
2. **Guest accounts with no sign-in for more than 90 days, or never: 2 accounts** (user-006, user-007). Outside people who may no longer work with you can still reach company information.
3. **Enabled, licensed accounts with no sign-in for more than 90 days: 2 accounts** (user-001, user-002). They still hold paid licences. They are also open doors that no one is using. Reclaimable cost: 230 synthetic units per month.

**Cost findings**

4. **Disabled accounts that still hold a licence: 1 account** (user-003). A licence is paid for, but the account cannot be used. Reclaimable cost: 100 synthetic units per month.
5. **Licensed members who never signed in, created more than 30 days ago: 1 account** (user-008). A paid licence has never been used. Reclaimable cost: 60 synthetic units per month.

**Total reclaimable cost:** 390 synthetic units per month, the sum of findings 3 to 5. These units were invented for this demo. They are not real prices.

2. Remove access for the two guest accounts, user-006 and user-007? Yes or no.
